Design Thinking Principles

1. DFM / DFA From Day One

Prioritizing standard tool clearances, modular sub-assemblies, and minimized weld distortion to reduce shop-floor machining hours by up to 35%.

2. Dual FEA & Physical Proofing

Validating digital Von Mises stress simulations against real-life hydraulic strain gauge tests to guarantee safety under 1.5x dynamic shock loads.

3. Real-Time Site Commissioning Agility

Rapid on-site redesign and redline adaptations to resolve unforeseen civil foundation variations and equipment interface misalignments.
